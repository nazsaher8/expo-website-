If You Decide to Report

A few practical steps can help you organize information.

🧾

Keep Relevant Records

When appropriate, preserve relevant messages, screenshots, dates or other information that may help explain what happened.

📝

Write Down Details

Record useful details while they are fresh, such as when and where an incident occurred and who may have been involved.

🔐

Protect Your Privacy

Store sensitive information carefully and share it only with appropriate trusted people, organizations or professionals.

Start With Someone You Trust

You do not have to make every decision immediately. If you feel comfortable doing so, consider telling someone you trust what is happening. They may help you think through your options and connect you with appropriate resources.

💙 Remember

You can choose how and when to seek support. If someone does not respond helpfully, consider reaching out to another trusted person or an appropriate professional service.

🆘 If You Are in Immediate Danger

Prioritize getting to a safer place when possible. Contact an appropriate local emergency service, security service or trusted person according to your circumstances.

Important

HerShield is an awareness and information project. It does not replace emergency services, medical care, counseling or professional legal advice.

Your Voice Matters.

Whether you need information, someone to listen, reporting guidance or another form of support, taking the first step can begin with learning about your options.
